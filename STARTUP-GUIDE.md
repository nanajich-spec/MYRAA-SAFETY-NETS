## BUSINESS REGISTRATION

### Step 1: GST Registration

**When:** Within 30 days of starting business (if turnover expected >₹40 lakhs/year)

**Where:** https://www.gst.gov.in

**Documents Needed:**
- Aadhar card
- PAN card
- Bank account details
- Electrical bill/rent agreement (address proof)
- Business address proof

**Cost:** Free

**Time:** 3-5 days online

**Benefits:**
- Legal compliance
- Input tax credit
- Professional credibility

### Step 2: MSME/Udyam Registration

**When:** Immediately after starting

**Where:** https://udyamregistration.gov.in

**Documents Needed:**
- Aadhar number
- Business name
- Business address
- Bank account details
- Type of business

**Cost:** Free

**Time:** 15 minutes online

**Benefits:**
- Government support
- Bank loans eligibility
- Tender bidding rights
- Subsidy schemes

### Step 3: Business License

**Where:** Municipal Corporation (Local municipal office)

**Cost:** ₹500-2000/year

**Time:** 1-2 weeks

**Benefits:**
- Legal operation permit
- Local area credibility

### Step 4: Professional Liability Insurance

**Where:** Any insurance company (online)

**Cost:** ₹3000-5000/year

**Benefits:**
- Customer protection
- Professional credibility
- Liability coverage

### Timeline Summary
```
Week 1: GST Registration + Udyam Register
Week 2: Business License + Insurance
Week 3: All documents in place
```

```
Medium (75-150 sqft):   ₹8,000 - ₹12,000
Large (150+ sqft):      ₹12,000 - ₹18,000
Premium Grade:          Add 15-20%
```

#### Pigeon Safety Nets
```
Small (50-100 sqft):    ₹3,000 - ₹5,000
Medium (100-200 sqft):  ₹5,000 - ₹8,000
Large (200+ sqft):      ₹8,000 - ₹12,000
```

#### Child Safety Nets
```
Standard installation:  ₹7,000 - ₹10,000
```

```
Quotation based on:
- Area covered
- Height of building
- Duration needed
- Access difficulty
Typical: ₹20,000+
```
