Welcome to Myraa Safety Nets & Invisible Grills

Myraa Safety Nets is one of the leading safety net and invisible grill installation companies in Hyderabad. We have years of experience in balcony safety nets, invisible grills, anti bird nets, pigeon nets, duct area safety nets, fall protection nets, building construction safety nets, coconut fall protection nets and sports nets.

We manufacture, supply and install all types of safety nets and invisible grills across Hyderabad for residential, commercial and industrial spaces. We bring clarity in design with timely delivery, transparent pricing and long-term support for every client.

Our invisible grills are made from high-tensile, rust-free stainless steel cables that keep your balconies and windows safe without blocking light, airflow or your view. From child safety and pigeon protection to modern invisible grills, Myraa is your trusted single-stop partner for complete home and building safety.
